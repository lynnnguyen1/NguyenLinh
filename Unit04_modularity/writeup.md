Write up - Unit 4 

Climate fluctuates drastically over decades and influences human life expectancy across the world. However, climate also changes differently across locations within a country, but the magnitude of this variation remains unclear. We examine annual temperature and precipitation from different weather stations in the USA from 1950 to 2008. 

Overall, in most locations, there are more records of increases in precipitation and temperature over the years. Visually inspecting, annual temperature has increased in some stations in Florida, Louisiana, and coast areas, but in Mississippi and Arizona, more stations were on a decreasing trend. Interestingly, Hawaii and nearby islands experienced different trends with some islands have the increase but some have the decrease temperature. Precipitation records were mostly from Ohio, Alabama and Tennessee, and they have increased over time, but there is also the mixed of trends within these states. 

However, there is an imbalance between two climatic variables, with about 12 times as many records for temperature as for precipitation. Besides, precipitation was reported mostly in only three states, which would not provide a clear examination for the USA. Besides, annual records do not quantify season-specific temperature changes, which might mask out changes in climate to some degree. 

Regardless, this report provides an interesting insight of climatic changes in USA over 50 years. Fluctuations in climate, especially temperature and precipitation, can influence life's expectancy drastically, hence, understanding its magnitude is crucial.
